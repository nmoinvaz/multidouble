\section{The proofs in Lean}\label{sec:lean}

The statements of these appendices are proved in Lean~4~\cite{Lean4} with Mathlib~\cite{Mathlib}, on
FloatSpec~\cite{FloatSpec}, a port of Flocq~\cite{BM11} that supplies the rounding and the theorems of $\TwoSum$ and
$\FastTwoSum$. The proofs are in \texttt{paper/lean/} of the repository~\cite{md}. Table~\ref{tab:lean} gives the
files of \texttt{ExpansionBounds/} and the theorems for each statement.
\begin{itemize}
\item The proofs hold for every precision $p \ge 2$. Lemmas~\ref{lem:red} and~\ref{lem:lnb}, Theorem~\ref{thm:trig}
and the constants at $n = 8$ are stated at $p = 53$.
\item A constant is evaluated by Lean's kernel on a copy of its recursion over the rationals, which a lemma equates
with the recursion over the reals (\texttt{Eval.lean}, \texttt{DivEval.lean}, \texttt{SqrtEval.lean}). Where exact
values grow too long the copy rounds up, which the bounds of Lemma~\ref{lem:g} allow because they grow with their
arguments (\texttt{GMono.lean}, \texttt{TrigEval.lean}, \texttt{ExpEval.lean}). The longest runs are cut into steps
that the kernel checks against stored states (\texttt{Prop28/}, \texttt{Trig8/}, \texttt{Tan8/} and \texttt{Exp8/},
written by \texttt{acc\_lean.py}, \texttt{trig\_lean.py} and \texttt{exp\_lean.py}).
\item No declaration depends on an axiom other than \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and
\texttt{Quot.sound}: the build reads the axioms of every declaration (\texttt{Controls/Axioms.lean}). Each of the
kernel's checks has controls, the same check on a wrong number, a wrong state or a narrower hypothesis, which the
kernel proves false (\texttt{Controls/}).
\item Three statements are proved in part. Theorem~\ref{thm:t8} takes the published bounds on its double-word
start~\cite{JMP17,LLMPR23,MR22} as hypotheses. Theorem~\ref{thm:log} is proved for each case, and its constant at
$n = 8$ is computed (\texttt{explog\_consts.py}). Proposition~\ref{prop:ddexplog} is proved for its start.
\end{itemize}
