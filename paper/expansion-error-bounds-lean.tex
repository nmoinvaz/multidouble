\section{The proofs in Lean}\label{sec:lean}

The statements of these appendices are proved in Lean~4~\cite{Lean4} with Mathlib~\cite{Mathlib}, on
FloatSpec~\cite{FloatSpec}, a port of Flocq~\cite{BM11} that supplies the rounding and the theorems of $\TwoSum$ and
$\FastTwoSum$. The proofs are in \texttt{paper/lean/} of the repository~\cite{md}. Tables~\ref{tab:lean}
and~\ref{tab:lean2} give the files of \texttt{ExpansionBounds/} and the theorems for each statement.
\begin{itemize}
\item The proofs hold for every precision $p \ge 2$, except that Lemmas~\ref{lem:red} and~\ref{lem:lnb},
Theorem~\ref{thm:trig}, Propositions~\ref{prop:ddexplog} and~\ref{prop:ratio}, the octo-double part of
Corollary~\ref{cor:mulrecip} and the constants at $n = 8$ are stated at $p = 53$, Proposition~\ref{prop:ddmul} and the
double-double part of Corollary~\ref{cor:mulrecip} from $p = 3$, and each published double-word theorem from its own
precision, 3 to 7.
\item A constant is evaluated by Lean's kernel on a copy of its recursion over the rationals, which a lemma equates
with the recursion over the reals. Where exact values grow too long the copy rounds up, which the bounds of
Lemma~\ref{lem:g} allow because they grow with their arguments. The longest runs are cut into steps
that the kernel checks against stored states, in folders of modules that the files of the tables import:
\texttt{Prop28/}, \texttt{Trig8/}, \texttt{Tan8/}, \texttt{Exp8/}, \texttt{Log8/}, \texttt{DDExp2/},
\texttt{DDLog2/} and, for the remark after Theorem~\ref{thm:trig} on the definitions of \texttt{TrigN/},
\texttt{Trig2/}, \texttt{Trig4/} and \texttt{Trig12/}, the last outside the default build, as the kernel takes some
five hours for it.
\item No declaration depends on an axiom other than \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and
\texttt{Quot.sound}: the build reads every declaration's axioms (\texttt{Controls/Axioms.lean}). Each kernel check
has controls, the same check on a wrong number, state or hypothesis, which the kernel proves false
(\texttt{Controls/}).
\item The published bounds under Theorem~\ref{thm:t8}'s double-word start, Theorem~\ref{thm:trig}'s
double-double functions and Proposition~\ref{prop:ddexplog} are proved as well, each for the algorithm used: those
of Theorems~2.2, 3.1, 4.1, 4.3 and~7.1 of~\cite{JMP17}, which \cite{MR22} proved in Coq, of Theorems~2.7 and~2.8
of~\cite{MR22} and of Theorem~3.5 of the preprint of~\cite{LLMPR23}. The quotient $x_l/x_h$ of the logarithm's start
enters by its bound $u|x_l/x_h| + \eta_0$, exact for $x_h = 1$.
\item The theorems at $n = 8$ on the exponential and the logarithm, and Proposition~\ref{prop:ddexplog}, take as
hypotheses binary64's rounding of each scaling and the exponents that the algorithm selects, and no value overflows,
the model having no largest number. The second step of their scaling by $2^k$ does not scale up; multidouble's does
for $k > 0$, exactly in binary64. A binary64 run must also meet (A2$'$). The
logarithm's constant is proved on the convex variant of its bound, at the two ends of each case, from the start that
Proposition~\ref{prop:ddexplog} computes.
\end{itemize}
