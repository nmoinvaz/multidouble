\section{The proofs in Lean}\label{sec:lean}

The statements of these appendices are proved in Lean~4~\cite{Lean4} with Mathlib~\cite{Mathlib}, on
FloatSpec~\cite{FloatSpec}, a port of Flocq~\cite{BM11} that supplies the rounding and the theorems of $\TwoSum$ and
$\FastTwoSum$. The proofs are in \texttt{paper/lean/} of the repository~\cite{md}. Table~\ref{tab:lean} gives the
files of \texttt{ExpansionBounds/} and the theorems for each statement.
\begin{itemize}
\item The proofs hold for every precision $p \ge 2$, except that Lemmas~\ref{lem:red} and~\ref{lem:lnb},
Theorem~\ref{thm:trig}, Proposition~\ref{prop:ddexplog} and the constants at $n = 8$ are stated at $p = 53$, and
each published double-word theorem from its own precision, 3 to 7.
\item A constant is evaluated by Lean's kernel on a copy of its recursion over the rationals, which a lemma equates
with the recursion over the reals (\texttt{Eval.lean}, \texttt{DivEval.lean}, \texttt{SqrtEval.lean}). Where exact
values grow too long the copy rounds up, which the bounds of Lemma~\ref{lem:g} allow because they grow with their
arguments (\texttt{GMono.lean}, \texttt{TrigEval.lean}, \texttt{ExpEval.lean}). The longest runs are cut into steps
that the kernel checks against stored states (\texttt{Prop28/}, \texttt{Trig8/}, \texttt{Tan8/}, \texttt{Exp8/},
\texttt{Log8/}, \texttt{DDExp2/}, \texttt{DDLog2/}, and \texttt{Trig2/} and \texttt{Trig4/} for the remark after
Theorem~\ref{thm:trig}, on the definitions of \texttt{TrigN/}, written by \texttt{acc\_lean.py},
\texttt{trig\_lean.py}, \texttt{exp\_lean.py},
\texttt{log\_lean.py}, \texttt{ddexp\_lean.py} and \texttt{ddlog\_lean.py}). That remark's bounds at $n = 12$ are computed
(\texttt{trig\_lean.py}), not proved: the kernel would need some eight hours for them.
\item No declaration depends on an axiom other than \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and
\texttt{Quot.sound}: the build reads the axioms of every declaration (\texttt{Controls/Axioms.lean}). Each of the
kernel's checks has controls, the same check on a wrong number, a wrong state or a narrower hypothesis, which the
kernel proves false (\texttt{Controls/}).
\item The published theorems under the double-word start of Theorem~\ref{thm:t8}, under the double-double
functions of Theorem~\ref{thm:trig} and under Proposition~\ref{prop:ddexplog} are proved as well: Theorems~2.2,
3.1, 4.1, 4.3 and~7.1 of~\cite{JMP17}, which \cite{MR22} proved in Coq, Theorems~2.7 and~2.8 of~\cite{MR22} and
Theorem~3.5 of~\cite{LLMPR23}. Proposition~\ref{prop:ddexplog} and the double-double sine, cosine and tangent are
proved on those algorithms; the quotient $x_l/x_h$ of the logarithm's start enters by its bound $u|x_l/x_h| +
\eta_0$, exact for $x_h = 1$.
\item The theorems at $n = 8$ on the exponential and the logarithm, and Proposition~\ref{prop:ddexplog}, take as
hypotheses binary64's rounding of each scaling and the exponents that the algorithm selects, and no value overflows,
the model having no largest number. The logarithm's constant is proved on the convex variant of its bound, at the
two ends of each case, with its start computed by Proposition~\ref{prop:ddexplog} (\texttt{Log8DD.lean}).
\end{itemize}
